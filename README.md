# Rebound

A roguelike about a bouncing ball. Your ball ricochets around the arena on its own, swinging its weapon at everything it hits.

**Play it in your browser:** visit the GitHub Pages site for this repository.

## How a run works

- **Pick a weapon:** Sword, Twin Daggers, Spear, Hammer, Bow or Bare Fists. Each plays differently and builds up its own bonus (its Ramp) during a fight.
- **Fight through 8 tiers.** Each tier has two fights and a boss with its own rules. Beat the last boss to win, then keep going in endless mode.
- **Shop between fights.** Spend your gold on augments, permanent stat upgrades and single-use items. Save gold to earn interest.
- **Build your ball.** Augments go into sockets and trigger from top to bottom, so their order matters. Damage is Power × Mult: stack the right pieces and the numbers get very big.
- **Gamble on the Night Market.** A rare Crystal Ball lets you pick one powerful Void augment, each with a big drawback.
- **Take a trophy** after every boss.

## Controls

| Key or action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Space or Enter | Start the fight |
| 1, 2, 3, 4 | Game speed 1×, 2×, 4×, 8× |
| P | Pause |
| Hold R | Restart the run |
| Drag an augment | Reorder your sockets, or drop it on the needle to sell |
| Right-click an augment | Sell it |
| Click your ball | Show your stats |

Hover over any coloured word on a card to see what it means.
